Employee Handbook

Working Hours:
The standard working day is 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
Monday through Friday. Employees should coordinate
with their managers if they need a schedule change.

Leave Policy:
Employees must request planned leave through the
company's leave management system. Managers review
leave requests based on team availability.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely according to their
team's remote work policy. Employees should contact
their manager for details about eligibility.

IT Support:
Employees who need technical assistance should
contact the IT support team through the internal
help desk portal.
